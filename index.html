RFP PHASE 2:

In Action Creative only response

From human evidence to a creative leap

First up. Thanks for selecting Federation for Phase 2. We’re genuinely excited to show you how we would apply our thinking to your business challenge. And very optimistic about what we’d achieve together.

The challenge you gave us is to sign up 26 more apartment settlements in six months. The problem isn't a lack of interest. Around 219 people tour these villages every quarter and 27 settle. People are looking, visiting, then putting the decision off.

That’s why we made ‘Deferral Unlock’ our number one strategic task and our approach starts there: what's stopping them, what could get them moving, and what needs to be done to turn interest into action.

We’ve written our own research brief, the brief we’d write anyway, to understand what's really behind decisions and decision deferral, what people are worried about, what finally prompts the move, and the role family plays.
